Every article in the [editor](/guides/editor) is scored live for two audiences: traditional **search engines** and modern **AI answer engines**. The analysis runs in the right sidebar and turns "is this good?" into a checklist of specific, fixable items.

## SEO vs. GEO

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="SEO — Search Engine Optimization" icon="magnifying-glass">
    Helps your article rank in Google and Bing. Looks at things like keyword usage, headings, meta description, title, links, and readability.
  </Card>
  <Card title="GEO — Generative Engine Optimization" icon="robot">
    Helps AI answer engines (like ChatGPT and AI Overviews) understand and *cite* your content. Rewards clear structure, direct answers, and fact-rich, well-organized passages.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Optimizing for both means your content can win traffic from classic search *and* be surfaced by the AI tools more people now use to find answers.

## Reading the analysis

The analysis presents a list of checks, each with a traffic-light status:

- 🟢 **Green** — passing
- 🟠 **Orange** — could be improved
- 🔴 **Red** — needs attention

Checks cover things like:

- Whether your **focus keyword** appears in the title, headings, intro, and meta description
- **Meta description** length and quality
- **Heading structure** and content organization
- **Readability** — passive voice, sentence length, paragraph length
- Presence of **internal and outbound links**
- **Image** usage and alt text

An overall **content score** summarizes how well the article is optimized.

Many checks can be resolved in one click. Any fixable item shows a **magic-wand icon (✨)** — clicking it opens a reviewable suggestion you can accept or reject.

## How to work with the score

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set your focus keyword">
    In the right sidebar, set the focus keyword so the analysis knows what to optimize for. Without it, keyword checks can't run.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Resolve red items first">
    Red checks are the most impactful. Work through them, then tidy up the orange ones.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Don't chase a perfect score">
    Aim for a strong score, but never sacrifice clarity or accuracy to satisfy a check. A natural, helpful article beats a keyword-stuffed one.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  GEO rewards content that answers the question directly and early. Lead sections with a clear, self-contained answer, then expand — this helps both skimming readers and AI engines that extract concise responses.
</Tip>

<Note>
  The analysis is a guide, not a gate. You can publish at any score — the checks are there to help you make the article as discoverable as possible.
</Note>
