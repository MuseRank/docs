The Knowledge Base lets you give MuseRank information and rules that go beyond what it learned from your website. Add your proprietary facts, product details, and editorial standards once, and they shape every article MuseRank generates — so the output reflects *your* expertise, not just general knowledge.

Find it under **Create → Knowledge Base** in the sidebar.

## Two kinds of entry

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Directives (rules)" icon="bullhorn">
    Editorial rules that are **always applied** to every article — your style and policy guardrails. For example: "Always use British spelling," "Never recommend competitor products," or "Refer to the product as MuseRank, never Muse Rank."
  </Card>
  <Card title="Facts (knowledge)" icon="book">
    Pieces of knowledge — product specs, statistics, company details, definitions — that are **pulled in by relevance** when an article's topic relates to them.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

The difference matters: **directives apply universally**, while **facts are retrieved only when relevant** to what you're writing about.

## Adding an entry

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Knowledge Base">
    Go to **Knowledge Base** in the sidebar and click **Add entry**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the type and scope">
    Mark it as a global **directive** (always applied) or a topical **fact** (applied by relevance).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write it clearly">
    Give it a clear title and concise body. Specific, unambiguous entries work best.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a priority">
    Higher-priority entries take precedence when guidance overlaps.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Managing entries

- **Enable / disable** — toggle an entry on or off without deleting it.
- **Priority** — ordering that decides which guidance wins when entries overlap.
- **Conflict warnings** — if two directives appear to contradict each other, MuseRank flags them. Both are still applied, with the higher-priority one taking precedence — so it's worth resolving real conflicts.
- **Status** — facts are made searchable in the background (embedded for relevance matching). An entry marked **needs re-embedding** isn't searchable yet; it's retried automatically.
- **Entry limit** — each website has a cap on the number of entries, shown as a count indicator. Delete entries you no longer need to free up space.

<Note>
  The Knowledge Base is scoped per **website**, so each site can carry its own facts and rules.
</Note>

## How it shapes your content

When MuseRank generates an article, it:

1. **Applies every enabled directive** as a hard rule for tone, terminology, and policy.
2. **Retrieves relevant facts** related to the article's topic and weaves them in.

This grounds the writing in your real expertise and keeps it on-brand — a strong complement to the [brand voice](/onboarding) MuseRank learned during onboarding.

<Tip>
  Start with a handful of high-value entries: your top 3–5 non-negotiable editorial rules as directives, and your most-cited product facts or statistics as facts. You can always add more as you notice patterns you want to enforce.
</Tip>
