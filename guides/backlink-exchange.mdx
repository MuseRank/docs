The Backlink Exchange is an opt-in network where participating MuseRank sites link to one another's relevant content. Links are one of the strongest ranking signals in SEO, and the exchange gives you a structured, safety-first way to earn relevant ones — while keeping you fully in control.

<Info>
  Participation is entirely opt-in and can be switched off for a domain at any time. Nothing is placed without your consent.
</Info>

## How it works

The exchange runs on **credits**. When the network places a relevant link to one of your pages, and when your content hosts a link to another member, credits are accounted for between the participants. Over time, this lets you build relevant inbound links from other real sites in the network.

Links are matched for relevance — the goal is genuinely useful links between related content, not random link spam.

## Safety comes first

Search engines penalize manipulative link schemes, so MuseRank's defaults are deliberately conservative:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Sponsored by default" icon="shield-check">
    Every new domain joins in **safe mode**, where exchange links use the `rel="sponsored"` attribute. This is the transparent, policy-aligned way to mark incentivized links — and it means the default never tries to manipulate rankings.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Velocity limits" icon="gauge">
    The network caps how many links are placed per source over time and enforces cooldowns, so growth looks natural rather than spammy.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Quality floors" icon="filter">
    Links are only placed between sites that meet quality and safety checks, including a domain-rating floor.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Diversity rules" icon="diagram-project">
    Caps prevent any single source from dominating a page's inbound links.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Warning>
  You can opt into higher-risk modes (mixing in or using standard "dofollow" links) from the exchange settings, but only after acknowledging the policy trade-offs. If you're unsure, stay in the default safe mode.
</Warning>

## You stay in control

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Turn it off anytime" icon="power-off">
    A domain-level switch lets you disable participation entirely. Pending placements are cancelled and associated credits refunded.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Remove links freely" icon="trash">
    If you edit a published article and remove an exchange link, you're never penalized — the credits are refunded and no link is forced back in. Editorial control over your own content is absolute.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="No silent dofollow" icon="eye">
    Links never silently pass ranking authority. Switching away from the sponsored default is an explicit, acknowledged choice.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Getting started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Backlink Exchange">
    Select it from the sidebar for your current website.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enroll your domain">
    Opt your domain into the network. It starts in safe mode by default.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review settings">
    Confirm your compliance mode and limits in the exchange settings. The defaults are the safe choice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build credits and earn links">
    As your content participates, you accrue credits and the network places relevant links over time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The exchange is designed around Google's guidance on incentivized links: by default, every exchange link is marked as sponsored. This keeps your site on the safe side of search-engine link policies.
</Note>
