## Writing and formatting

The editor is a rich-text surface — type naturally and format as you go.

- **Slash commands** — type `/` on a new line to insert headings, lists, quotes, code blocks, images, and more.
- **Inline formatting** — select text to apply bold, italic, links, and other styles.
- **Headings** — structure your article with H1–H6. Good heading structure helps both readers and your [SEO score](/guides/seo-geo-analysis).

<Tip>
  Clear heading structure isn't just cosmetic — it's one of the strongest signals for both search ranking and AI answer engines. Use the outline to organize before you polish prose.
</Tip>

## Autosave

Your work saves automatically as you write. A save indicator shows the current status, so you never have to remember to hit save. If you navigate away and come back, your latest content is there.

## Version history

The editor keeps a history of your article's versions. If a round of edits goes the wrong way — or an AI change you accepted didn't land well — you can review earlier versions and restore one. This makes it safe to experiment.

<Tip>
  Made a change you regret right away? The standard undo shortcut (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>/<kbd>Cmd</kbd> + <kbd>Z</kbd>) reverses your most recent edits instantly.
</Tip>

## Metadata and publishing

In the right sidebar you'll set the details that matter for search and publishing:

- **SEO title** and **meta description** — what shows up in search results.
- **Slug** — the URL-friendly path for the article.
- **Focus keyword** — the term the analysis and AI Assistant optimize for.
- **Featured image** — the cover image carried to your CMS.

When everything looks good, publish directly or send the article to the [Scheduler](/guides/scheduler).

<Note>
  Metadata you set here travels with the article when you publish to a connected platform — see the [Integrations overview](/integrations/overview) for what each destination supports.
</Note>
