Auto-Pilot runs your whole content engine for you. Once configured, it continuously finds opportunities, writes articles in your brand voice, and publishes them to your connected platforms — turning MuseRank into a hands-off content machine.

## The loop

<Steps>
  <Step title="Seed topics" icon="seedling">
    Auto-Pilot draws topic ideas from your website and your [topical map](/guides/topical-maps).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Research keywords" icon="magnifying-glass">
    It runs [keyword research](/guides/keyword-research) on those seeds to find winnable opportunities — not random topics.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate articles" icon="pen-nib">
    For each winnable keyword, it generates a complete, SEO/GEO-optimized article in your [brand voice](/onboarding).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish automatically" icon="paper-plane">
    Finished articles are published to your connected CMS on the cadence you set.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Because research happens before generation, every Auto-Pilot article targets a keyword you can realistically rank for — this is what separates it from generic bulk content tools.

## Set it up

Auto-Pilot is configured **per website**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect a publishing destination">
    Auto-Pilot needs somewhere to publish — connect at least one [integration](/integrations/overview) first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Auto-Pilot settings">
    Find Auto-Pilot in your website's settings and enable it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the cadence and sources">
    Choose how often it should publish and let it draw from your topical map and site for topics.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let it run">
    Auto-Pilot works in the background, publishing on schedule. Review what it produces in [Articles](/guides/editor) and [Search Performance](/guides/search-performance).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Auto-Pilot consumes credits as it generates articles. Keep an eye on your [credit balance](/guides/billing-and-credits) and set a cadence that fits your plan's allowance so you're not surprised by running out.
</Warning>

## Staying in control

Auto-Pilot is automation, not abdication. You can:

- **Review before it's live** — combine Auto-Pilot with the [Scheduler's approval workflow](/guides/scheduler#approvals-and-guest-review) so a human signs off first.
- **Edit anything** — every Auto-Pilot article is a normal article you can open and refine.
- **Pause anytime** — turn Auto-Pilot off whenever you want.

<Tip>
  Build out a solid [topical map](/guides/topical-maps) before turning on Auto-Pilot. The better your map, the more strategic and connected its output will be.
</Tip>
