The Topical Map Studio is where you turn a pile of keywords into a *strategy*. Instead of publishing scattered, unrelated posts, you map out connected clusters of content that establish your site as an authority on a subject — which is what search engines reward.

## Why topical authority wins

Search engines favor sites that cover a topic comprehensively. If you publish one article about a subject, you're a single data point. If you publish a **pillar** page plus a cluster of supporting articles that link to each other, you signal genuine expertise — and the whole cluster ranks better than any single post would alone.

The Topical Map Studio helps you design that structure deliberately.

## Build your map

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Topical Map Studio">
    Select **Topical Map** in the sidebar. You'll see an interactive canvas of your topics.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your core themes">
    Start from the keywords in your [Keyword Bank](/guides/keyword-bank) or add topics directly. Group related ones into clusters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Designate pillars">
    Within each cluster, mark the broad, high-value topic as the pillar. The narrower, supporting topics become cluster articles that link up to it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Plan internal links">
    Connect related nodes so MuseRank knows how articles should link to one another when published.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate from the map">
    Turn any node into an article — MuseRank carries the keyword, cluster context, and internal-linking plan into the [article wizard](/guides/article-wizard).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Internal linking

Internal links are central to topical authority. MuseRank uses your map to suggest and insert links between related articles, so a reader (and a search crawler) can move naturally from a supporting article to its pillar and across the cluster. You can also let the [AI Assistant](/guides/ai-assistant) add relevant internal links while you edit.

<Tip>
  Don't try to map your entire site at once. Pick one cluster, publish it fully, and measure results in [Search Performance](/guides/search-performance) before expanding.
</Tip>

## From map to published cluster

<Steps>
  <Step title="Map a cluster">
    Define one pillar plus its supporting topics.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate the articles">
    Create the pillar and supporting drafts, each in your brand voice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link them together">
    Ensure supporting articles link to the pillar and to each other where relevant.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule the rollout">
    Use the [Scheduler](/guides/scheduler) to publish the cluster over a sensible cadence.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  When you publish to a CMS like WordPress, MuseRank can map a cluster's pillar to a category, keeping your published structure aligned with your plan.
</Note>
