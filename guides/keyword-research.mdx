Keyword Research is where strategy starts. Instead of writing about whatever comes to mind, you find the specific search terms you can realistically rank for — and build your content around them.

## The idea: target winnable keywords

A keyword is **winnable** when it has meaningful search demand *and* competition you can actually beat. Chasing high-volume head terms dominated by major brands wastes effort; targeting winnable terms gets you traffic faster.

MuseRank scores every keyword on a **winnability score** from 0 to 100 that blends two signals:

- **Search volume** — how many people search for the term each month.
- **Keyword difficulty** — how hard it is to rank against the current competition.

Low difficulty plus solid volume produces a high score.

<Info>
  Keyword difficulty and volume data come from a professional SEO data provider, so scores reflect real market conditions — not guesses.
</Info>

## How to read the winnability score

| Score | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **70–100** | Excellent opportunity — low difficulty with good traffic. Prioritize these. |
| **50–69** | Good opportunity with reasonable competition. Strong candidates. |
| **30–49** | Moderate — workable, but expect more effort to rank. |
| **Below 30** | Challenging — usually better to find an easier angle. |

As a rule of thumb, **start with keywords scoring 50 or above**.

## Run a keyword search

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Keyword Research">
    Select **Keyword Research** in the sidebar for your current website.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a seed topic">
    Type a topic related to your business — for example, `headless cms`, `email marketing for ecommerce`, or `dog training`. MuseRank expands it into many related keyword ideas.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the results">
    Results appear in a table with each keyword's **search volume**, **difficulty**, and **winnability score**, sorted so the best opportunities rise to the top.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Act on the good ones">
    For any promising keyword you can:
    - **Save to Keyword Bank** to keep it for later planning.
    - **Generate Article** to start writing immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Turn a keyword into an article

Click **Generate Article** next to any keyword to launch the [article wizard](/guides/article-wizard) with that keyword pre-loaded as the target. MuseRank builds the brief, outline, and draft around it and optimizes the content to rank.

<Tip>
  Found several winners on one theme? Save them all to your [Keyword Bank](/guides/keyword-bank), then organize them into clusters in the [Topical Map Studio](/guides/topical-maps) before you start writing. Clustered content builds authority faster than one-off posts.
</Tip>

## Tips for stronger research

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Use specific, intent-rich seeds" icon="bullseye">
    Long-tail seeds like `best crm for solopreneurs` surface more winnable, conversion-ready keywords than broad seeds like `crm`.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Look for question and comparison terms" icon="circle-question">
    "How to", "vs", and "best" style keywords often have clear intent and beatable competition — great for winnable wins.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Connect Google Search Console first" icon="chart-line">
    With [GSC connected](/integrations/google-search-console), MuseRank can also surface opportunities based on terms your site already *almost* ranks for — some of the easiest wins available.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
