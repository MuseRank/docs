The Keyword Bank is your running shortlist of keywords worth targeting. It's the bridge between *finding* opportunities and *acting* on them — a place to collect the best terms before you commit them to articles.

## What lives in the keyword bank

Any keyword you save from across MuseRank lands here:

- Winnable keywords saved from [Keyword Research](/guides/keyword-research)
- Keywords pulled in from your [Topical Map](/guides/topical-maps)
- Opportunities surfaced from [Google Search Console](/guides/search-performance)

Each saved keyword keeps its metrics — search volume, difficulty, and winnability — so you can prioritize at a glance.

## How to use it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Collect keywords">
    As you research, save promising keywords to the bank instead of acting on each one immediately. This lets you build a strategic backlog.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and prioritize">
    Open **Keyword Bank** from the sidebar. Sort by winnability to see your strongest opportunities first and weed out terms that no longer fit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn keywords into articles">
    When you're ready to write, generate an article directly from a saved keyword. MuseRank launches the [article wizard](/guides/article-wizard) with the keyword as the target.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Think of the keyword bank as your content backlog. A healthy bank means you always have a winnable topic ready to write — no staring at a blank page.
</Tip>

## A typical flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Research a theme">
    Run a few [keyword searches](/guides/keyword-research) around your core topics.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bank the winners">
    Save every keyword scoring 50+ to the bank.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cluster them">
    Group related saved keywords into a [topical map](/guides/topical-maps) so your articles reinforce each other.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write on a schedule">
    Pull from the bank steadily, or feed it into [Auto-Pilot](/guides/autopilot) to generate and publish automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Keywords in the bank are scoped to the website you're working in. Switch websites to see that site's own keyword bank.
</Note>
