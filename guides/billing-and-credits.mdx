MuseRank runs on a subscription plus a **credit** system for AI work. This page explains how the pieces fit together so there are no surprises.

## Plans

MuseRank offers three plans, billed **monthly** or **quarterly** (quarterly is discounted):

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Starter" icon="seedling">
    For individuals and small sites getting started with consistent content.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Growth" icon="chart-line">
    The most popular plan — the best balance of output, automation, and team workflows for most teams.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Done For You" icon="briefcase">
    For agencies and multi-site teams publishing at higher volume.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Each plan differs by:

- **Articles (credits) per billing cycle** — your AI generation allowance.
- **Number of websites** — how many separate sites you can manage.
- **Workflow and support level** — higher plans unlock more automation and support.

<Note>
  Exact prices, credit amounts, and website limits are shown live on the pricing screen in the app, since they can change. Always check the in-app pricing for current numbers.
</Note>

## Credits

AI generation runs on **credits**, where one credit is roughly **one AI-generated article**.

- Your plan includes a credit allowance that **resets each billing cycle**.
- Generating an article through the [wizard](/guides/article-wizard) or [Auto-Pilot](/guides/autopilot) uses credits.
- Smaller AI helpers — like the [AI Assistant's](/guides/ai-assistant) quick fixes — are covered by your subscription under fair-use limits and don't burn a full credit.

### Running out of credits

If you reach your limit before the cycle resets, MuseRank prompts you with two options:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Buy a credit pack" icon="cart-shopping">
    A one-time purchase that tops up your balance immediately — handy for a busy month without committing to a bigger plan.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Upgrade your plan" icon="arrow-up">
    Move to a higher tier for a larger recurring allowance (and more websites).
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  If you publish in bursts, the quarterly billing option pools a larger credit allowance up front, which can be more flexible than a monthly reset.
</Tip>

## Starting and managing your subscription

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a trial">
    New accounts can start on a low-cost short trial to explore the product before committing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a plan">
    Choose Starter, Growth, or Done For You and a billing period. Checkout is handled securely by our payment provider.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage anytime">
    From the **Billing** page in the sidebar you can upgrade, downgrade, switch billing periods, buy credit packs, update payment details, and view invoices.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Plan changes

- **Upgrades** generally take effect immediately so you get the larger allowance right away.
- **Downgrades and cancellations** typically take effect at the end of your current billing period, so you keep what you've paid for until then.

<Note>
  Billing is an **owner-only** capability. If you're an admin or editor and need a plan change, ask your workspace owner. See [Workspaces & roles](/guides/workspaces-and-roles).
</Note>

For invoices, refunds, or anything unclear, reach out — see [Support](/help/support). Common billing questions are also covered in the [FAQ](/help/faq).
