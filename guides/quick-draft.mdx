Quick Draft is the fastest way to start a new article. It skips the four-step [wizard](/guides/article-wizard) and drops you straight into a blank [editor](/guides/editor), ready to type.

## When to use Quick Draft

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="You already know what to write" icon="bolt">
    No need for a generated brief or outline — just start.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Short or simple posts" icon="feather">
    Announcements, notes, or pieces that don't need the full guided flow.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Pasting existing content" icon="clipboard">
    Bringing in a draft you wrote elsewhere to polish in MuseRank.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Power users" icon="gauge-high">
    You want the shortest path from idea to editor.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How to start a quick draft

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Articles">
    Go to **Articles** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Quick Draft">
    Next to **New Article**, choose **Quick Draft**. A blank article is created and the editor opens immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write">
    Start typing. Use slash commands (`/`) for formatting, and the [AI Assistant](/guides/ai-assistant) or [Article Chat](/guides/article-chat) whenever you want help.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Quick Draft vs. the wizard

| | Quick Draft | Article Wizard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Starting point** | Blank editor | Generated full draft |
| **Steps** | None — straight to writing | Keyword → Brief → Outline → Draft |
| **Best for** | Quick posts, pasting, power users | SEO-targeted articles from scratch |
| **Keyword targeting** | You add it yourself | Built in from step one |

<Tip>
  Even in a quick draft, you can set a focus keyword in the editor sidebar so the [SEO & GEO analysis](/guides/seo-geo-analysis) and AI Assistant know what to optimize for.
</Tip>
