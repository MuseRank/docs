The AI Assistant turns the warnings in your [SEO & readability analysis](/guides/seo-geo-analysis) into one-click fixes. Instead of rewriting passages by hand, you click a magic-wand icon, review the suggestion, and accept it if it's good.

## How it works

As you write, the analyzer in the editor's right sidebar checks your article and flags issues with traffic-light indicators:

- 🟢 **Green** — looks good
- 🟠 **Orange** — could be improved
- 🔴 **Red** — needs attention

Any issue the AI can fix shows a **magic-wand icon (✨)** next to it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find a fixable issue">
    Look for orange or red checks with a ✨ icon and click the wand ("Fix with AI").
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the suggestion">
    The assistant generates an improvement — usually within a few seconds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the change">
    A diff opens showing your original text on one side and the suggestion on the other, with additions and deletions highlighted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept or cancel">
    Click **Accept Changes** to apply it, or cancel to leave your text untouched. Accepted changes apply instantly to your content or metadata.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You're the editor-in-chief. Always read a suggestion before accepting — the AI is fast and usually right, but you know your audience and facts best.
</Tip>

## What the AI Assistant can fix

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Meta description" icon="rectangle-list">
    Writes a compelling 120–155 character meta description that includes your focus keyword and earns clicks from search results.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Passive voice" icon="arrow-right-arrow-left">
    Rewrites passive sentences into active voice for clearer, punchier reading. *"The button was clicked by the user" → "The user clicked the button."*
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Sentence complexity" icon="scissors">
    Breaks long, dense sentences into shorter ones that are easier to read.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Internal links" icon="link">
    Finds relevant phrases and links them to your other articles with natural anchor text — strengthening your [topical authority](/guides/topical-maps).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Outbound links" icon="arrow-up-right-from-square">
    Adds helpful links to authoritative sources and relevant videos where they support your content.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## A good fixing order

For the best results, work top-down:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start with the meta description">
    It's what searchers see first, so it has outsized impact.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fix readability">
    Resolve passive voice and complex sentences so the writing is clear.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add links last">
    Once the prose is solid, layer in internal and outbound links as a final polish.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Good to know

- **Fair-use limits** — quick fixes are rate-limited (around five per minute) to keep things responsive. If you hit the limit, wait a moment and try again.
- **It's included** — the AI Assistant comes with your subscription; quick fixes don't burn a full article credit.
- **Undo anytime** — if you accept a change and change your mind, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>/<kbd>Cmd</kbd> + <kbd>Z</kbd> reverses it.
- **Don't over-optimize** — not every suggestion needs accepting. Keep what sounds natural and on-brand.

<Note>
  The AI Assistant is tuned for English content today. For free-form help — rewrites, expansions, or research — use [Article Chat](/guides/article-chat).
</Note>
