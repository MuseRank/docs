The article wizard is the guided way to create content in MuseRank. It walks you from a target keyword to a finished first draft in four steps, keeping you in control at each stage while doing the heavy lifting in your brand voice.

<Note>
  Prefer to skip the structure and just start typing? Use [Quick Draft](/guides/quick-draft) instead.
</Note>

## The four steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Keyword" icon="key">
    Choose the target keyword for the article. If you launched the wizard from [Keyword Research](/guides/keyword-research), the [Keyword Bank](/guides/keyword-bank), or a [topical map](/guides/topical-maps), it's already filled in. This keyword anchors the SEO optimization for the whole piece.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Brief" icon="clipboard-list">
    MuseRank proposes a brief — the angle, target reader, and intent for the article. Review it and adjust anything that doesn't match your goal. A good brief keeps the draft focused.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Outline" icon="list-tree">
    Next comes a structured outline of headings and subtopics. Add, remove, or reorder sections so the article covers exactly what you want. This is your last checkpoint before writing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Draft" icon="pen-nib">
    MuseRank writes the full draft, streaming it in live. It follows your outline, targets your keyword, and matches the [brand voice](/onboarding) it learned from your site.
  </Step>
</Steps>

When the draft is ready, it opens in the [editor](/guides/editor) for refinement.

## Start the wizard

You can launch it from several places:

- **Articles → New Article** in the sidebar.
- The **Generate Article** button next to any keyword in Keyword Research or the Keyword Bank.
- A node in the [Topical Map Studio](/guides/topical-maps).
