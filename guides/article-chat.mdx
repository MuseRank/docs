Article Chat is a conversational assistant built into the [editor](/guides/editor). Where the [AI Assistant](/guides/ai-assistant) handles specific, predefined fixes, Article Chat is open-ended — ask it anything about your article and it responds in context, including pulling in fresh information from the web.

## What you can ask it

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Rewrite and rephrase" icon="pen-to-square">
    "Make this intro punchier" or "rewrite this section for a beginner audience."
  </Card>
  <Card title="Expand and add" icon="plus">
    "Add a section on common mistakes" or "give me three more examples here."
  </Card>
  <Card title="Research a topic" icon="globe">
    "What are the latest stats on X?" — Article Chat can search the web for current information.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Summarize and explain" icon="lightbulb">
    "Summarize this article" or "explain this concept more simply."
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Live web research

A key strength of Article Chat is that it isn't limited to what a model already knows. When you ask about current facts, recent developments, or external sources, it can search the web and bring relevant findings into the conversation — useful for adding up-to-date data points and citing sources in your article.

<Tip>
  Always verify facts and figures the assistant brings back before publishing them. Treat web research as a fast first draft of your sources, not the final word.
</Tip>

## How to use it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Article Chat in the editor">
    With an article open, open the chat panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask in plain language">
    Describe what you want. The assistant has the context of your current article, so you can refer to "this section" or "the intro."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply what's useful">
    Bring suggested text into your draft and refine it. You stay in control of what actually lands in the article.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Article Chat vs. AI Assistant

| | Article Chat | [AI Assistant](/guides/ai-assistant) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Style** | Open-ended conversation | One-click, predefined fixes |
| **Best for** | Rewrites, expansions, research, questions | Resolving specific SEO/readability warnings |
| **Web research** | Yes | No |
| **Where** | Chat panel in the editor | Magic-wand icons on analysis checks |

<Note>
  Use Article Chat to shape and grow your content, and the AI Assistant to clean up the specific issues flagged by the [SEO & GEO analysis](/guides/seo-geo-analysis).
</Note>
