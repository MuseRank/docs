Search Performance shows you how your content is actually doing in Google — clicks, impressions, rankings, and trends — and turns that data into new opportunities. It's powered by your [Google Search Console](/integrations/google-search-console) connection.

## Connect Google Search Console first

Search Performance needs data, and that comes from Google Search Console (GSC). Connecting GSC is the **recommended first integration** in MuseRank because it powers insights across the whole app — keyword research, content audit, and this dashboard.

<Card title="Connect Google Search Console" icon="chart-line" href="/integrations/google-search-console">
  A quick OAuth flow links your verified GSC property to MuseRank.
</Card>

## Turning data into action

The most valuable part of Search Performance is the **opportunities** it surfaces. Some of the easiest traffic gains come from keywords where you already rank on, say, page two — a focused refresh or a few internal links can move you onto page one.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Spot near-miss keywords">
    Look for queries where you rank just outside the top results but already get impressions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Strengthen the page">
    Open the ranking article and improve it — expand a thin section, sharpen the focus keyword, or add [internal links](/guides/topical-maps#internal-linking).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save new targets">
    Send promising opportunity keywords to your [Keyword Bank](/guides/keyword-bank) to build into future content.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refresh decliners">
    Use the [Content Audit](/guides/content-audit) to catch articles losing ground and update them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Check Search Performance regularly to close the loop: publish → measure → refine. Content that you monitor and improve almost always outperforms content you publish and forget.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Search Console data updates on Google's schedule (typically with a couple of days' delay), so very recent publishes may take a little time to appear.
</Note>
