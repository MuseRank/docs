The Scheduler is your content calendar and publishing queue. It's where articles move from idea to live — with visual planning, an optional approval workflow, and reliable automated publishing.

## Scheduling content

### Drag and drop

Drag an article onto a date (month view) or a time slot (week view) to schedule it. Drag an item out of the **backlog** panel — your unscheduled content — onto the calendar to give it a slot. Reschedule anytime by dragging it somewhere else.

### Queues — automatic scheduling

A **queue** is a recurring posting schedule. Instead of placing each article by hand, you define the days and times you want to publish (and your timezone), then add articles to the queue — each one automatically takes the next open slot.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a queue">
    Define your posting days and times and set your timezone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add articles">
    Drop finished articles into the queue; they fill the next available slots in order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let it run">
    Articles publish automatically at their assigned times.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Queues are the easiest way to keep a consistent publishing cadence — set "Tuesday and Thursday at 9am" once and just feed the queue.
</Tip>

## The workflow states

Each article moves through a pipeline so you always know where it stands:

```
Idea → Brief → Draft → Review → Approved → Scheduled → Published
```

If a publish fails, it moves to **Failed** and is retried automatically (see below). During review, an editor can send a piece back for changes.

## Approvals and guest review

If your team requires sign-off before publishing, turn on the **approval workflow**:

- Content must be **approved** before it can publish.
- Reviewers can **approve**, **request changes**, or **unapprove**, and every action is recorded in an audit trail.
- **Guest review links** let you share an article with someone outside your workspace (a client or stakeholder) so they can review and approve without needing an account.

<Note>
  Approving, requesting changes, and generating guest links are **admin** actions. Editors create and submit content; admins approve it. See [Workspaces & roles](/guides/workspaces-and-roles).
</Note>

## Reliable publishing

When an article's scheduled time arrives, MuseRank publishes it to your connected destination automatically.

- **Preflight checks** validate the connection and required fields before publishing.
- If a publish fails (for example, a temporary CMS outage), it's **retried automatically** with increasing delays.
- You can always **Publish now** for an immediate push, or **Schedule…** for a specific future time.
