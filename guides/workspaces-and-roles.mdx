A workspace is your team's shared home in MuseRank. This guide covers inviting members, what each role can do, and how permissions shape collaboration.

## Workspaces and websites

Your **workspace** holds your team, your billing, and one or more **websites**. Each website has its own content, brand voice, and integrations. Most teams use a single workspace; agencies managing many clients may create several.

Switch between websites from the top of the sidebar — the app reloads to show that site's data.

## Roles

Every member holds exactly one role. Roles are simple and additive — a higher role can do everything a lower one can, plus more.

| Role | Can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Everything. Manage billing, delete the workspace, manage API keys, change member roles, plus all admin and editor abilities. The workspace creator is the first owner. |
| **Admin** | Manage members and invitations, connect/disconnect and configure integrations, edit website settings and brand voice, and approve content — plus all editor abilities. |
| **Editor** | Create, edit, delete, and publish articles, and use already-connected integrations. The day-to-day content role. |

<Note>
  There is no separate "viewer" or "writer" role. Editor is the baseline content role — there's no view-only tier. The role set is exactly **owner, admin, editor**.
</Note>

### Who can do what

| Action | Owner | Admin | Editor |
| --- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| Create / edit / publish articles | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Use connected integrations | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Approve content & guest links | ✓ | ✓ | — |
| Connect / configure integrations | ✓ | ✓ | — |
| Invite & manage members | ✓ | ✓ | — |
| Edit website settings & brand voice | ✓ | ✓ | — |
| Manage billing | ✓ | — | — |
| Manage API keys | ✓ | — | — |
| Change member roles | ✓ | — | — |
| Delete website or workspace | ✓ | — | — |

<Note>
  Sensitive actions like deleting a website or workspace or removing a member may ask you to re-confirm your password for security, even if you're already signed in.
</Note>

## Inviting teammates

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open member settings">
    As an owner or admin, go to your workspace settings and find the members section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send an invitation">
    Enter the person's email and choose their role (admin or editor).
  </Step>
  <Step title="They accept">
    The invitee gets an email with a link to join your workspace.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Approval workflows

When editors and admins work together, you can require sign-off before anything goes live:

- Editors create and submit content.
- Admins **approve**, **request changes**, or **unapprove** — all tracked in an audit trail.
- **Guest review links** let external stakeholders (like clients) review and approve without an account.

This is configured in the [Scheduler](/guides/scheduler#approvals-and-guest-review).

## Signing in

MuseRank supports several sign-in methods, and members can choose what suits them:
