Great articles need images. MuseRank lets you add a **featured image** (the cover) and images within the body, sourced from Unsplash's free library or your own uploads — and handles optimization for you.

## Featured images

The featured image is your article's cover. It appears at the top of the post and is what most CMS platforms and social previews use as the thumbnail.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the featured image picker">
    In the editor's right sidebar, find the **Featured image** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a source">
    Either search the built-in **Unsplash** library for a relevant, license-free photo, or **upload** your own image.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set it">
    Select the image to set it as the article's cover. It's optimized automatically and travels with the article when you publish.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Images inside the article

To place an image in the body, use the slash command (`/`) where you want it and choose the image option, then pick from Unsplash or upload a file. Images are processed and stored by MuseRank so they load fast wherever you publish.

## Sourcing options

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Unsplash" icon="camera">
    Search a large library of free, high-quality, commercially usable photos without leaving the editor.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Your uploads" icon="upload">
    Upload your own screenshots, diagrams, product shots, or brand imagery.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## What happens on publish

When you publish to a connected platform, MuseRank carries your images across and handles the platform's requirements automatically — for example, uploading the featured image to your WordPress Media Library or Shopify Files so it displays correctly on your live site.

<Note>
  Image handling varies slightly per destination. See each integration's page in [Publishing & Integrations](/integrations/overview) for specifics.
</Note>

<Tip>
  A relevant featured image improves click-through from search and social. Pick something that reflects the article's topic rather than a generic stock cliché.
</Tip>
