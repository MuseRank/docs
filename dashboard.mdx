The dashboard is the first screen you see when you open a website. It pulls together your performance metrics, suggested next actions, and automation status so you always know where things stand and what to do next.

<Note>
  Everything on the dashboard is scoped to the **currently selected website**. Switch websites from the top of the sidebar to see another site's dashboard.
</Note>

## What's on the dashboard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Command bar">
    Across the top: a greeting, your **Auto-Pilot** status (including a countdown to the next scheduled run when it's enabled), a refresh button, and a **New Article** button that opens the [article wizard](/guides/article-wizard).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Status banner">
    A single contextual banner surfaces the most important thing right now — for example, a setup step to finish, Auto-Pilot activity, new opportunities, or [content plan updates to review](/guides/content-plan#reviewing-plan-updates).
  </Step>
  <Step title="KPI strip">
    Four key metrics at a glance: **Clicks**, **Impressions**, **Average position**, and your content **Pipeline**. The search metrics come from [Google Search Console](/integrations/google-search-console), and each card is clickable to drill in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Work grid">
    A **Do Today** list of suggested next actions, alongside a **context rail** highlighting opportunities — like keywords you're close to ranking for that are worth an article.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## First-time setup

On a brand-new website with no content yet, the dashboard shows a focused **empty state** that points you to your first action: creating an article. A short **guided tour** also runs the first time, pointing out the sidebar, the workspace switcher, and the New Article button.

<Tip>
  Connect [Google Search Console](/integrations/google-search-console) early. Until you do, the KPI strip and opportunity suggestions have no data to show — GSC is what makes the dashboard genuinely useful.
</Tip>
