MuseRank is an AI content and SEO platform. It helps you find keywords you can realistically rank for, generate articles in your brand's voice, and publish them to your CMS — keeping research, writing, and publishing in one place.

It's designed for content marketers, founders, agencies, and SEO teams who want to publish consistent, search-optimized content without a large writing team.

## What you can do with MuseRank

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Find keywords you can actually win" icon="magnifying-glass">
    MuseRank scores keyword opportunities by combining search volume and difficulty into a single **winnability score**, so you spend your effort on terms you can realistically rank for — not impossible head terms.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Build topical authority" icon="sitemap">
    The Topical Map Studio organizes your topic into clusters and pillar pages with internal links, so search engines see your site as an authority on the subject.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Generate articles in your brand voice" icon="pen-nib">
    During onboarding, MuseRank learns your writing style from your existing site. Every article it drafts matches that voice — then you refine it in a full-featured editor with an AI assistant.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Plan and automate publishing" icon="calendar">
    Schedule content on a calendar, run an approval workflow, or hand the whole loop to Auto-Pilot — research, generation, and publishing on a recurring schedule.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Publish everywhere" icon="paper-plane">
    Push finished articles to WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, Framer, Ghost, GitHub, Notion, or any custom endpoint via webhooks — with images, SEO metadata, and scheduling carried across.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Track and grow" icon="chart-line">
    Connect Google Search Console to see how your content performs, surface new opportunities, and earn relevant links through the Backlink Exchange.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## How the workflow fits together

The typical MuseRank journey moves in one direction — from research to results:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up your brand voice">
    During [onboarding](/onboarding), MuseRank analyzes your site to learn how you write and what you're about.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Research winnable keywords">
    Use [Keyword Research](/guides/keyword-research) to find low-competition terms with real traffic, and save the best ones to your [Keyword Bank](/guides/keyword-bank).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Plan your topics">
    Map your keywords into clusters and pillars with the [Topical Map Studio](/guides/topical-maps).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create content">
    Generate an article with the [article wizard](/guides/article-wizard), then polish it in the [editor](/guides/editor).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule and publish">
    Queue articles in the [scheduler](/guides/scheduler) or let [Auto-Pilot](/guides/autopilot) run the loop, then publish to your [connected platforms](/integrations/overview).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Measure and improve">
    Track results in [Search Performance](/guides/search-performance) and refresh aging content with the [Content Audit](/guides/content-audit).
  </Step>
</Steps>
