## About MuseRank

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is MuseRank?" icon="circle-question">
    MuseRank is an AI content and SEO platform. It helps you research winnable keywords, plan topical authority, generate brand-matched articles, and publish them to your CMS — all in one place. See the [introduction](/introduction).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who is it for?" icon="users">
    Content marketers, founders, agencies, and SEO teams who want to publish consistent, search-optimized content without building a large writing team.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to install anything?" icon="download">
    No. MuseRank runs in your browser at [app.muserank.com](https://app.muserank.com). The only optional install is the WordPress plugin if you publish to WordPress.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Content & AI

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Will the content sound like AI?" icon="comment">
    MuseRank learns your [brand voice](/onboarding) from your existing site and applies it to every draft, so output matches your tone. You always refine in the [editor](/guides/editor) before publishing.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is the content unique?" icon="fingerprint">
    Yes — each article is generated for you around your specific keyword, brief, and outline. You're always the editor-in-chief and can revise anything.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is my content used to train AI models?" icon="shield">
    No. Your content is used to generate suggestions and articles for you — not to train models or share with others.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What languages are supported?" icon="language">
    MuseRank is optimized for English content today. Support for more languages is on the roadmap.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What's the difference between the wizard and Quick Draft?" icon="pen-nib">
    The [article wizard](/guides/article-wizard) guides you through Keyword → Brief → Outline → Draft for SEO-targeted pieces. [Quick Draft](/guides/quick-draft) drops you into a blank editor to start writing immediately.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Credits & billing

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is a credit?" icon="coins">
    A credit is roughly one AI-generated article. Your plan includes an allowance per billing cycle, and smaller AI helpers like quick fixes don't use a full credit. See [Billing & credits](/guides/billing-and-credits).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens when I run out of credits?" icon="circle-exclamation">
    You can buy a one-time credit pack for an instant top-up, or upgrade your plan. Credits also reset at the start of each cycle.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change or cancel my plan?" icon="arrows-rotate">
    Yes. From the **Billing** page you can upgrade, downgrade, switch billing periods, or cancel. Upgrades generally apply immediately; downgrades and cancellations apply at the end of your current period.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do quick fixes cost credits?" icon="wand-magic-sparkles">
    No — the [AI Assistant's](/guides/ai-assistant) quick fixes are included with your subscription under fair-use limits.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Publishing & integrations

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where can I publish?" icon="paper-plane">
    WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, Framer, Ghost, Notion, GitHub, and any custom endpoint via [webhooks](/integrations/webhooks). See the [integrations overview](/integrations/overview).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I publish to more than one platform?" icon="layer-group">
    Yes — connect multiple destinations per website and choose where each article publishes.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will re-publishing create duplicates?" icon="copy">
    No. Re-publishing updates the existing post on your CMS rather than creating a new one.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I schedule content?" icon="calendar">
    Yes. Use the [Scheduler](/guides/scheduler) for a calendar and recurring queues, or [Auto-Pilot](/guides/autopilot) to automate research, writing, and publishing.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Data & SEO

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What makes a keyword 'winnable'?" icon="trophy">
    A high [winnability score](/guides/keyword-research) means a keyword has real search volume *and* beatable competition — so you can realistically rank for it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is GEO?" icon="robot">
    Generative Engine Optimization — optimizing content so AI answer engines (like ChatGPT and AI Overviews) can understand and cite it. MuseRank scores both SEO and GEO. See [SEO & GEO analysis](/guides/seo-geo-analysis).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I have to connect Google Search Console?" icon="chart-line">
    It's optional but strongly recommended — GSC powers performance tracking, keyword opportunities, and a sharper content audit. See [Google Search Console](/integrations/google-search-console).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is the backlink exchange safe?" icon="shield-check">
    By default, exchange links use the `rel="sponsored"` attribute (the policy-aligned, transparent choice), with velocity limits and full editorial control. See [Backlink exchange](/guides/backlink-exchange).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Account & team

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I invite my team?" icon="user-plus">
    Yes. Owners and admins can invite members as admins or editors. See [Workspaces & roles](/guides/workspaces-and-roles).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What are the roles?" icon="user-shield">
    Owner, admin, and editor. Owners control billing and the workspace, admins manage members and integrations and approve content, and editors do day-to-day content work.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I manage multiple websites?" icon="globe">
    Yes — add multiple websites (subject to your plan's limit), each with its own brand voice, content, and integrations.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
