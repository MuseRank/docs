We're here to help. Whether you're stuck, have a billing question, or want to suggest an improvement, here's how to reach us.

## Ways to get help

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="In-app chat" icon="comments">
    The fastest way to reach us. Open the support chat from inside MuseRank to message the team directly.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Email" icon="envelope" href="mailto:support@muserank.com">
    Prefer email? Write to **support@muserank.com** and we'll get back to you.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Share feedback" icon="lightbulb">
    Use the **Feedback** option in the app to request features or report issues. We read everything.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Search the docs" icon="magnifying-glass" href="/help/faq">
    Many questions are answered in the [FAQ](/help/faq) and [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting) guides.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Before you reach out

To help us resolve things faster, it's useful to include:

<Steps>
  <Step title="What you were doing">
    The feature and the action you took (for example, "publishing an article to WordPress").
  </Step>
  <Step title="What you expected vs. what happened">
    Including any error message you saw, word for word if possible.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The affected website">
    Which of your websites the issue is on, and the integration involved if relevant.
  </Step>
  <Step title="A screenshot">
    A screenshot of the screen or error often speeds things up.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Check whether your question is covered in [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting) first — common issues like reconnecting an integration or topping up credits have quick self-serve fixes.
</Tip>
