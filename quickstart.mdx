This guide takes you through the full MuseRank flow — creating an account, setting up your brand voice, finding a keyword, generating an article, and publishing it. Plan for about 15–20 minutes.

<Note>
  Everything here happens in the MuseRank app at [app.muserank.com](https://app.muserank.com). No installation required.
</Note>

## Step 1 — Create your account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up">
    Go to [app.muserank.com](https://app.muserank.com) and create an account with your email and a password, or continue with **Google** or **GitHub**. You can also add a [passkey](/guides/workspaces-and-roles#signing-in) later for faster sign-in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your email">
    Click the link in the verification email we send you. This unlocks the rest of the app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a plan">
    Pick a plan to start your trial. Plans differ by how many articles (credits) and websites they include — you can change or cancel anytime. See [Billing & credits](/guides/billing-and-credits).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Step 2 — Set up your brand voice

After subscribing, MuseRank walks you through a short **onboarding** that defines the website you'll create content for.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add your website">
    Enter your site's URL. MuseRank visits it to understand what you do.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let MuseRank learn your voice">
    MuseRank analyzes your existing pages to learn your tone, style, and audience, then builds a reusable **brand voice profile**. Every article it generates will match this.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and confirm">
    Check the details MuseRank extracted, adjust anything that's off, and finish onboarding.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For a full walkthrough, see [Onboarding & brand voice](/onboarding).

## Step 3 — Connect a data source (recommended)

Before researching, connect **Google Search Console**. It's the recommended first integration because it powers keyword opportunities and performance tracking across the app.

<Card title="Connect Google Search Console" icon="chart-line" href="/integrations/google-search-console">
  Open **Integrations → Google Search Console** and authorize your site. This step is optional but highly recommended.
</Card>

## Step 4 — Find a winnable keyword

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Keyword Research">
    From the sidebar, go to **Keyword Research**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a seed topic">
    Type a topic related to your business (for example, `email marketing for ecommerce`) and run the search.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a high-winnability keyword">
    Review the results sorted by **winnability score**. Aim for keywords scoring 50+ — they have real traffic and beatable competition. Save promising ones to your [Keyword Bank](/guides/keyword-bank).
  </Step>
</Steps>

Learn more in [Keyword Research](/guides/keyword-research).

## Step 5 — Generate your first article

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the article wizard">
    From a keyword, click **Generate Article**, or open **Articles → New Article**. The wizard guides you through four steps: **Keyword → Brief → Outline → Draft**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review each step">
    Confirm the target keyword, adjust the brief and outline, then let MuseRank write the draft. It streams in live and matches your brand voice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Polish in the editor">
    The draft opens in the [editor](/guides/editor). Use the [AI Assistant](/guides/ai-assistant) to fix any flagged SEO or readability issues, add a [featured image](/guides/images), and check your [SEO & GEO score](/guides/seo-geo-analysis).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  In a hurry or already know what you want to write? Use [Quick Draft](/guides/quick-draft) to skip the wizard and start with a blank editor.
</Tip>

## Step 7 — Publish or schedule

<Steps>
  <Step title="Publish now">
    From the editor, click **Publish** and choose your connected destination. MuseRank carries across the title, content, slug, featured image, and SEO metadata.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or schedule it">
    Prefer to plan ahead? Send the article to the [Scheduler](/guides/scheduler) to publish at a future date, or set up a recurring queue.
  </Step>
</Steps>
