The first time you set up a website, MuseRank runs a short onboarding that does one important thing: it learns **how you write** so every article it generates sounds like you, not like generic AI.

## Why brand voice matters

AI-generated content often reads flat and interchangeable. MuseRank avoids this by studying your existing website first and capturing a **brand voice profile** — a reusable summary of your tone, vocabulary, audience, and subject matter. That profile is then applied to every draft, so the output is consistent with your brand from the very first article.

<Note>
  The brand voice profile is created per **website**. If you manage multiple sites, each one gets its own voice.
</Note>

## What happens during onboarding

<Steps>
  <Step title="You add your website URL">
    Enter the address of the site you want to create content for. This anchors the whole website in MuseRank.
  </Step>
  <Step title="MuseRank analyzes your site">
    MuseRank visits a sample of your existing pages and reads how you write — your sentence style, formality, recurring themes, and who you're talking to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="It builds your brand voice profile">
    From that analysis, MuseRank produces a profile describing your tone, target audience, key topics, and style. This becomes the reference for all future generation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You review and adjust">
    Nothing is locked in. Review what MuseRank captured, correct anything that doesn't fit, and confirm. You can revisit and edit the profile later in your website settings.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tips for the best result

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Point MuseRank at content-rich pages" icon="file-lines">
    The more representative writing your site has — blog posts, guides, an about page — the more accurately MuseRank can capture your voice. A brand-new site with only a landing page gives it less to work with.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Refine the profile after the fact" icon="sliders">
    If early drafts feel slightly off, tweak the brand voice profile in your website settings. Small adjustments to tone or audience description carry through to every new article.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Set up a website per brand" icon="layer-group">
    Don't mix two different brands into one website. Create a separate website for each so their voices stay distinct.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
