Google Search Console (GSC) is the **recommended first integration** in MuseRank. It brings your real search data into the app, which powers performance tracking, keyword opportunities, and the content audit.

<Info>
  GSC is a *data source*, not a publishing destination. Connecting it makes the rest of MuseRank smarter — it doesn't change anything on your site.
</Info>

## Why connect it first

Much of MuseRank's intelligence improves once it can see how your site actually performs in Google:

- **[Search Performance](/guides/search-performance)** — clicks, impressions, rankings, and trends for your pages and queries.
- **Keyword opportunities** — terms you already *almost* rank for, which are often the easiest wins.
- **[Content Audit](/guides/content-audit)** — real performance data sharpens which articles truly need a refresh.

## What you'll need

- A Google account with access to a verified property in Google Search Console for your website.

## Connect Google Search Console

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the GSC integration">
    In MuseRank, go to **Integrations** for your website and find the Google Search Console card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize with Google">
    Click connect and sign in with the Google account that has access to your site's GSC property. Approve the read-only access MuseRank requests.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your property">
    Choose the verified GSC property that matches the website you're working on.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
  Once connected, head to [Search Performance](/guides/search-performance) to explore your data.
</Check>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My property isn't listed" icon="list-check">
    Make sure the Google account you authorized with has access to the property in Google Search Console, and that the property is verified.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Connection expired or needs reconnecting" icon="arrows-rotate">
    If the Integrations page flags GSC as expired or invalid, reconnect to refresh access.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="No data yet" icon="clock">
    Search Console data updates on Google's schedule, usually with a couple of days' delay. Newly verified properties or very recent publishes can take time to show data.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
