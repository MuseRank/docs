Connect Shopify to publish finished articles straight into your store's blog. MuseRank creates Shopify blog articles with your content, featured image, and SEO metadata.

## What you'll need

- A Shopify store with admin access.
- A plan that supports apps (all paid Shopify plans).
- Your store's `.myshopify.com` domain.

## Connect Shopify

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the connection">
    In MuseRank, open **Integrations** for your website, find the Shopify card, enter your store's `.myshopify.com` domain, and click **Connect Shopify**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve the permissions">
    Approve the Shopify authorization screen. MuseRank requests **content-only** scopes — blogs, articles, metafields, and files. It does not touch orders, customers, or payments.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a default blog">
    After connecting, open **Settings → Shopify** and pick the blog where articles should be published.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Enter your `.myshopify.com` address (for example, `example-store.myshopify.com`), not your custom storefront domain.
</Warning>

## Publishing

Once connected and configured, publish from the [editor](/guides/editor) or [Scheduler](/guides/scheduler). MuseRank:

- Uploads your featured image to **Shopify Files**.
- Creates a blog article with your HTML content and SEO metadata.
- Stores the resulting article URL back in MuseRank.

<Tip>
  Use the **Send test draft** action in the Shopify app to confirm everything is wired up before you publish for real.
</Tip>

## Republishing

Editing and re-publishing an article updates the existing Shopify blog article rather than creating a duplicate.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title='"Shop domain required"' icon="store">
    Enter the `.myshopify.com` domain, not a custom domain.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Switching stores" icon="arrows-rotate">
    Update the domain and reconnect to regenerate credentials for the new store.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Permission errors" icon="lock">
    These usually mean the app was removed inside Shopify. Reconnect from MuseRank to re-install it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
