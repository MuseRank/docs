---
title: Webflow
description: Publish MuseRank articles into a Webflow CMS collection, with custom field mappings and automatic updates on republish.
---

Connect Webflow to publish articles into a CMS collection on your Webflow site. You connect via OAuth, choose a site and collection, map your fields once, and MuseRank handles the rest.

## What you'll need

- A Webflow site with a CMS collection for your blog or articles (for example, a "Blog Posts" collection).
- Permission to authorize apps on your Webflow account.

## Connect Webflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Authorize Webflow">
    In MuseRank, open **Integrations** for your website, find the Webflow card, and connect. Approve the Webflow authorization screen — MuseRank requests access to read and write your sites and CMS.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a site and collection">
    Select the Webflow site and the CMS collection where articles should be published.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Map your fields">
    Match MuseRank's article fields (title, body, summary, image, and so on) to the fields in your Webflow collection. Save the mapping.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If you don't set custom mappings, MuseRank falls back to common defaults (such as `post-body` and `post-summary` field slugs). Mapping explicitly is recommended so every field lands where you expect.
</Note>

## Publishing

After setup, publish from the [editor](/guides/editor) or [Scheduler](/guides/scheduler). MuseRank creates a CMS item in your chosen collection using your field mappings.

- **Republishing** updates the existing CMS item in place (matched by article), so you won't get duplicates.
- An item's draft/published state reflects how it's configured in your collection.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Articles publish but fields are empty" icon="table-columns">
    Check your field mappings — an unmapped field won't receive content. Make sure your collection has a rich-text/body field mapped.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Authorization expired" icon="key">
    Reconnect Webflow from the Integrations page to refresh access.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Changes not visible on the live site" icon="globe">
    Webflow may require a site publish for CMS changes to appear on your live domain, depending on your setup.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
