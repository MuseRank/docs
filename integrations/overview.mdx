Integrations connect MuseRank to the rest of your stack — the places you **publish** content and the data sources that make MuseRank smarter. You manage them per website on the **Integrations** page in the app.

<Note>
  Integrations are scoped to each **website**. Connect them for the site you're working in, and switch websites to manage another site's connections.
</Note>

## Two kinds of integration

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Publishing destinations" icon="paper-plane">
    Where your finished articles go live. Connect one (or several) and publish or schedule articles straight to them — with images and SEO metadata carried across.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Data & marketing" icon="chart-line">
    Sources that feed MuseRank's insights and broadcast your publishes. Google Search Console is the most important — connect it first.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How publishing works

However your destination is set up, the flow is the same from your side:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect once">
    Authorize the destination on the Integrations page. Connecting and disconnecting integrations is an **admin** action.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure where needed">
    Some destinations need a choice — a default blog (Shopify), a collection (Webflow), or a repo and path (GitHub).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish or schedule">
    From the [editor](/guides/editor) hit **Publish**, or send the article to the [Scheduler](/guides/scheduler) for a future date. [Auto-Pilot](/guides/autopilot) can publish automatically too.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Republish safely">
    Editing and re-publishing an article **updates** the existing post rather than creating a duplicate.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What gets carried across

When you publish, MuseRank sends the full article and its metadata to the destination. Depending on what the platform supports, that includes:

- Title and full content
- URL slug
- Featured image (uploaded to the platform's media library where applicable)
- SEO title, meta description, and focus keyword
- Tags and category
- Status and scheduled date

<Tip>
  You can connect more than one destination per website and choose where to publish each article — handy if you syndicate content across platforms.
</Tip>
