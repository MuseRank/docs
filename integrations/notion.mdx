Connect Notion to publish MuseRank articles into a Notion database. This is useful as a collaborative review space, a content archive, or a hub that other tools read from.

## Connect Notion

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the connection">
    In MuseRank, open **Integrations** for your website and find the Notion card. Start the connection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize and choose a destination">
    Approve the Notion authorization screen and grant MuseRank access to the workspace and the database (or page) where articles should be created.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Notion only exposes the pages and databases you explicitly share with an integration during authorization. Make sure to grant access to the destination you want to publish into.
</Note>

## Publishing

Once connected, publishing or scheduling an article creates a corresponding entry in your Notion database with the article's content and metadata. Publishing happens reliably in the background, so your article appears in Notion shortly after you publish.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Articles aren't appearing in Notion" icon="file-circle-xmark">
    Confirm that the target database or page was shared with the MuseRank integration during authorization. Reconnect and re-grant access if needed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Authorization expired or invalid" icon="key">
    Reconnect Notion from the Integrations page to refresh access.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
